## Behavior and handling

List payer-created MED cases for the authorized account. This list excludes receiver-side notification cases and cases created through the administrative flow. Results are ordered by case opening time from newest to oldest. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · api-18-F07</Badge> Find a case and open its details page to check processing progress.

## Permissions and account ownership

Use `ACCOUNT` credentials for their bound account, or `USER` credentials with both `X-ISPB` and `X-Account-Number` for an account owned by that `USER` and within its authorized account hierarchy. `PLATFORM` credentials are not accepted. With `ACCOUNT` credentials, omit both target headers or provide both with values matching the bound account; partial, empty, or mismatched target headers are rejected. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · api-18-F13</Badge> Requests must follow [Environments & Credentials](/environments-and-credentials).

**Required:** No (not enforced at this layer). Inclusive lower bound for the case opening time, as a UTC Unix timestamp in milliseconds. Omitted, 0, and negative values do not apply this filter. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · api-18-F05</Badge>

**Required:** No (not enforced at this layer). Inclusive upper bound for the case opening time, as a UTC Unix timestamp in milliseconds. Omitted, 0, and negative values do not apply this filter. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · api-18-F06</Badge>
