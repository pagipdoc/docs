## User registration

Choose Onboarding or Merchant based on your integration entry point. After review and account activation are complete, configure API access.

- **Choose registration entry point**
   - Onboarding registration: Business information and related-person verification → Review → Activate account
   - Merchant registration: Create a sign-in user → Submit for account verification → Review
- **Prepare API integration**
   - After registering and activating your account, follow "Enable API access" below to complete the configuration
   - [Configure the environment, IP allowlist, and signature](/environments-and-credentials)
   - [Choose an account model](/account-models). If you need a managed account, use the [Onboarding API](/api-reference/onboarding/overview) to open it and confirm that it is ready

<Accordion title="Onboarding registration · View registration steps">
[Go to registration](https://banking-onboarding.pagsmile.com.br/onboarding)

<Steps>
<Step title="Prepare information before starting">
Make sure you have a mobile number and an email address that can receive verification codes. Submit the following information and documents as required by the application form. Use the file formats specified on the review page.

| Business information | Related persons' identity verification data |
| --- | --- |
| Full company name, CNPJ | Personal information and contact details |
| Business phone number, email address, and business address | Identity document that meets verification requirements |
| Articles of association (Contrato Social) and business registration information | Device suitable for selfie verification |
| Business activities, website, incorporation date, and estimated monthly processing volume | Residential address and proof of address |
| Supporting documents, ownership structure, and related persons' details | Entry point and required information for verifying your own identity |

Business verification (KYB) and related persons' identity verification are separate parts of the business account opening process. You must complete both as required by the application.
</Step>

<Step title="Register a business account">
- Open the Onboarding account opening portal and enter your mobile number, including the country code. Read the consent information and give the required consent, then click **Send Verification Code**. Enter the six-digit verification code from the text message.
- Enter the full company name, CNPJ, and email address. Check the information, complete the required confirmations, and click **Continue**.
- Click **Start KYB Verification**, then select **Start Verification**. On the AIPrise verification page, select a language and click **Get Started**.

<Step title="Complete business verification">
Complete the following seven sections in order. You must also review automatically extracted information. Entering business information manually does not waive the requirement to submit documents later.

| Section | Actions and information |
| --- | --- |
| Business information | Upload the articles of association to automatically fill out the form, or select Enter manually; fill in any missing required information |
| Business contact details | Enter the business phone number and email address |
| Business address | Enter the country, street, state, city, and postal code |
| Business operations and finances | Enter the entity type, business model, industry, business description, website, estimated monthly processing volume, and date of incorporation |
| Business documents | Upload the documents required by the form, such as a bank account opening letter or bank statement, proof of business address, and a company structure chart |
| Ownership structure and legal representative | Use **Add Related Person** to add related persons. Enter their roles, names, dates of birth, ownership interests, and contact details. After saving, click **Verify Now** to complete identity verification |
| Corporate shareholders | If there are corporate shareholders, use **Add Related Company** to add their details. Review the entire application and click **Submit** |

<Note>
Saving a related person's details does not mean their identity verification is complete. Submitting an individual for identity verification does not mean you have submitted the full business application.
</Note>

## Enable API access

After registration, review, and account activation are complete, sign in to the Banking platform. Configure MFA, API credentials, the IP allowlist, and webhooks.

<Accordion title="Enable API access · View setup steps">
<Steps>
<Step title="Sign in to the Banking platform">
Go to the [Banking platform](https://banking.pagsmile.com.br/login) and sign in with your phone number or CPF and password. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete sign-in MFA verification.
</Step>

<Step title="Set up account MFA">
Go to **Settings → Security** and click **Set Up Account MFA** in the **Payment MFA** section. <Badge color="orange" size="sm">Corrected · module-2-F02</Badge> Follow the on-screen instructions to link and verify account MFA to securely confirm payments and other operations.

<Frame caption="Account MFA setup in Settings">
<img src="/images/quickstart/account-mfa-setup-in-settings.png" alt="Account MFA setup in Settings" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Enable Open API">
Go to **API Keys** and turn on the **Open API** toggle in the **Bank Account Level Credentials** section. <Badge color="orange" size="sm">Corrected · module-2-F01</Badge> The Open API toggle is available to the account owner for a CNPJ account. Set up account MFA first. In the **Enable Open API** dialog, enter your Google Authenticator code and click **Enable**. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · module-2-F03</Badge>

<Frame caption="Enter your Google Authenticator code to enable Open API">
<img src="/images/quickstart/enter-your-google-authenticator-code-to-enable-ope.png" alt="Enter your Google Authenticator code to enable Open API" />
</Frame>
</Step>

Go to **API Keys** (page title: **API Keys and Webhooks**) and confirm the selected bank account in the **Bank Account Level Credentials** section. This section is separate from **USER LEVEL CREDENTIALS (OPENVA & ONBOARDING)**. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · module-2-F04</Badge> Click **Enable Access Key**, enter your Google Authenticator code, and click **Confirm**.

<Frame caption="Confirm the current bank account and enable Access Key">

</Step>

<Step title="Save credentials">
In the **New API Keys** dialog, copy and securely save your **Access Key (AK)** and **Secret Key (SK)**. The full credentials are displayed only once. After saving them, select **I have saved my keys** and click **Close**.

<Frame caption="Save API credentials, full keys masked">
<img src="/images/quickstart/save-api-credentials-full-keys-masked.png" alt="Save API credentials, full keys masked" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Configure IP allowlist">
Click the edit icon in the **Allow List** column of the credentials list. Enter the outbound server IP address used to send API requests, enter your Google Authenticator code, and click **Submit**.

<Frame caption="Edit Allow List and submit the verification code">
<img src="/images/quickstart/edit-allow-list-and-submit-the-verification-code.png" alt="Edit Allow List and submit the verification code" />
</Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Configure webhook">
Click **API Keys** in the left menu, then click **Add Webhook** in the **Webhooks** section.

- **App ID:** Select the application.
- **Event Type:** Select PAYMENT IN (incoming payment), PAYMENT OUT (outgoing payment), REFUND IN (incoming refund), or REFUND OUT (outgoing refund) as needed.
- **Callback URL:** Enter an accessible HTTPS callback URL.
- **Content Type:** Select `application/json` and click **Confirm** to save.

<Frame caption="Add a webhook: select an application, event, and callback URL">
<img src="/images/quickstart/add-a-webhook-select-an-application-event-and-call.png" alt="Add a webhook: select an application, event, and callback URL" />
</Frame>

Add each required event separately and verify the configuration in the list. For callback receipt and delivery rules, see [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks/overview). For signature verification rules, see [Environments & Credentials](/environments-and-credentials).
</Step>

<Step title="Get USER-level credentials">
To get USER-level credentials, contact Pagsmile to enable PSP functionality and confirm how to obtain the credentials and which permissions are available.
</Step>

<Step title="Configure USER-level credentials and webhook">
After PSP is enabled, click **API Keys** in the left menu and go to the **USER LEVEL CREDENTIALS (OPENVA & ONBOARDING)** section:

- Click **Enable Access Key** to get USER-level credentials. Securely save your AK/SK and configure the IP allowlist.
- Under **Webhooks** in this section, click **Add Webhook** and configure USER-level events and callback URLs as needed. Available events are `PAYMENT IN`, `PAYMENT OUT`, `REFUND IN`, `REFUND OUT`, `VA ACCOUNT KYB`, `MED PROCESSING`, `INFRACTION PROCESSING`, and `ACCOUNT STATUS UPDATE`. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · module-2-F11</Badge>

<img src="/images/quickstart/entry-point-for-user-level-credentials-and-webhook.png" alt="Entry point for USER-level credentials and webhook configuration" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

When finished, configure the environment and request signing as described in [Environments & Credentials](/environments-and-credentials). You can then start integrating API payments.

## Payment integration

The following shows typical integration flows for core operations. Click an endpoint name to view request parameters and examples.

- **Enable Pix**
   - [Create Pix key](/api-reference/pix-keys/create-pix-key) → [Get bank account and active Pix keys](/api-reference/accounts/get-account-balance); creating a key does not enable the Pix service.
- **Incoming payments: create order → generate QR code → confirm credit to the account**
   - [Generate dynamic QR code](/api-reference/payin/generate-dynamic-qr-code) → Display to customer → Customer pays
   - [Incoming payment callbacks](/api-reference/webhooks/transaction-callbacks)/[Get transaction details](/api-reference/payin/get-transaction-details) → Confirm final result → Update order
- **Outgoing payments: Initialize → Confirm → Get result**
   - [Initialize Pix key payment](/api-reference/payout/pix/initialize-pix-key-payment) → [Confirm Pix key payment](/api-reference/payout/pix/confirm-pix-key-payment) → [Outgoing payment callbacks](/api-reference/webhooks/transaction-callbacks)/[Get payment status](/api-reference/payout/get-payment-status) → Update payment record.
   - Confirm using the returned `ref_id` and the same account context. If initialization included an amount, the confirmation amount must match. For other payment methods, see [PayOut](/api-reference/payout/overview). Call the endpoints in the specified order. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · module-2-F14</Badge>
- **Refunds: link original incoming payment → initiate refund → confirm result**
   - Verify original incoming payment and refund conditions → [Initiate Pix refund](/api-reference/refunds/initiate-pix-refund)
   - [Refund callbacks](/api-reference/webhooks/transaction-callbacks)/[Get refund details](/api-reference/refunds/get-refund-details) → Update refund record
- **MED: Link original transaction → Create case → Track outcome**
   - [Get account statement](/api-reference/accounts/get-account-statement) → Verify original Pix transaction and case eligibility → [Create MED case](/api-reference/med/create-med-case)
