## Behavior and handling

Get managed account details to track account opening, compliance, and bank account information. Successful acceptance of an account opening request does not mean the account is ready for transactions.

### Track account opening and compliance status

| Account status | Meaning and action |
| --- | --- |
| PENDING | Account opening is in progress; continue querying |
| PENDING_SUBMISSION | This endpoint does not return `PENDING_SUBMISSION` as an account status. Awaiting information submission is reported by `compliance_status=2` (`COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_PENDING_SUBMISSION`); complete the information submission process. <Badge color="orange" size="sm">Corrected · api-37-F03</Badge> |
| ACTIVE | Account is activated; confirm bank account details and transaction permissions |
| FROZEN | Account is frozen; process transactions according to the restrictions for this status |
| CLOSED | Account is closed; do not use it for new funds operations |
| REJECTED | Account opening was rejected; check the reason and follow the review process |
| EXPIRED | The account opening process has expired; restart it using the applicable process |

Compliance status and account status are returned separately. The compliance process can be in PENDING, PENDING_SUBMISSION, REVIEWING, APPROVED, FORCE_APPROVED, REJECTED, EXPIRED, CREATION_FAILED, or ERROR. Record both statuses and `updated_at`. This response does not include a separate compliance-status update timestamp; `activated_at` is the compliance approval time. <Badge color="orange" size="sm">Corrected · api-37-F04</Badge>

## Permissions and account ownership

Use `USER` credentials without `X-ISPB` or `X-Account-Number` headers, including empty headers. The managed account must belong to the user associated with the credentials. The user must have exactly one valid `ACTIVE` MA with an active bank account. MOR and VA must belong directly to that MA. A PA must belong directly to that MA or to an `ACTIVE` VA under that MA, with an active bank account. Requests must follow [Environments & Credentials](/environments-and-credentials). <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · api-37-F01</Badge>

## Request parameters

<ParamField body="reference_id" type="string">
**Required:** Provide exactly one of the two. Provide a non-empty `reference_id` or the complete `account` object, not both. Leading and trailing whitespace is removed from `reference_id`, `account.ispb`, and `account.account_number` before validation and lookup. Values consisting only of whitespace are empty. No UUID format is required for `reference_id`. <Badge color="blue" size="sm">Added · api-37-F02</Badge>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account" type="object">
**Required:** Provide exactly one of the two. Provide either this object or reference_id, not both; ispb and account_number in the object must both be non-empty
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account.ispb" type="string">
↳ **Required:** Required when account is provided. Non-empty string; the bank participant identifier and account number must be provided together, with leading zeros preserved
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="account.account_number" type="string">
↳ **Required:** Required when account is provided. Non-empty string; the bank participant identifier and account number must be provided together, with leading zeros preserved
</ParamField>

KYC/KYB compliance status. Enum: 0 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_UNSPECIFIED; 1 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_PENDING; 2 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_PENDING_SUBMISSION; 3 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_APPROVED; 4 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_REJECTED; 5 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_EXPIRED; 6 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_CREATION_FAILED; 7 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_REVIEWING; 8 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_ERROR; 9 COMPLIANCE_STATUS_V3_FORCE_APPROVED.

Linked compliance process ID.

Email address for account notifications.

Creation time in UTC Unix milliseconds. Can be 0 if creation has not occurred or the time is not provided.
